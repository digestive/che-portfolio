  • Self-healing site navigation map

What counts as a testing hour
  • Generating or updating the site navigation map
  • Writing new tests or updating existing ones
  • Running automated test suites
  • Generating AI bug reports on failures
  • Hours reset monthly with no rollover. Extra hours beyond your limit are billed at your plan's per-hour rate.

Unlike consumption-based AI tools that charge per inference or checkpoint, your subscription covers all execution runs. AI costs are absorbed into your hourly rate.
